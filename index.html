Terms & Conditions

Apex Task Studio Rules

Please read carefully before working on tasks.

1. One Account Per Person

Each worker is allowed only ONE account. Multiple accounts using the same name, email, phone number, or M-Pesa number will be flagged and may result in account suspension and forfeiture of earnings.

2. Real Information Required

You must register with your real name, valid email, and a personal M-Pesa number in your own name. Fake identities or impersonation will lead to a permanent ban.

3. Quality Submissions

All work must be genuine, accurate, and completed by you. Copying, AI-generated content without disclosure, or submitting work that does not match the task will be rejected.

4. Task Reservations

When you start a task, it is reserved for 30 minutes. If you do not submit within that time, the task reopens for other workers.

5. Payouts

Payments are processed via M-Pesa within 24 hours of approval. Minimum withdrawal is $1.00. All payouts are subject to task approval by our QA team.

6. Account Suspension

Violation of any of these terms may result in the suspension or termination of your account without notice. Pending earnings may be forfeited in cases of fraud.
