Worker Help Center

How do I start completing tasks?

Browse available tasks under Active Work Assignments. Click "Start & Submit", complete the instructions, and paste your proof or output into the submission box.

When will my earnings be approved?

Submissions are reviewed by administrators within 12–24 hours. Once approved, your pending balance moves to your Approved Balance.

How does M-Pesa Cashout work?

Click the "Cashout" button on your Approved Balance card. Enter your withdrawal amount and M-Pesa phone number. Payouts are processed directly to your mobile wallet.

Need direct support?

Email our support team at support@apextaskstudio.com with your registered email and phone number.
