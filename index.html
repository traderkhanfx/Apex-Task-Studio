Frequently asked questions

📌 How do I get paid?

Once your task is approved by our QA team, the payout is added to your balance. You can withdraw via M-Pesa anytime.

📌 Why is a task locked?

Your first task is free. Premium tasks require a small unlock fee ($1.50–$2.00) to filter serious workers and maintain quality.

📌 What if my task is rejected?

You'll be notified with feedback. You can resubmit the corrected task. Repeated rejections may affect your ranking.

📌 How long do withdrawals take?

M-Pesa withdrawals are processed within 24 hours on business days.
