Help Center

Quick answers for Apex Task Studio workers.

How do I start a task?

Select an available task, press “Start & Submit,” complete the instructions, paste your proof, and submit it for review.

Why are some tasks locked?

Your first task is free. Additional work may require a one-time task unlock fee between $1.50 and $2.00, deducted from your available balance.

How do M-Pesa withdrawals work?

Open the withdrawal form, confirm your registered M-Pesa number, enter an amount within your available balance, and submit the request.
